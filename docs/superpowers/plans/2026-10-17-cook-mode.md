# Cook Mode, Scaling, Units and Substitutions Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Cook from the phone: the screen stays awake, there is a step-by-step view with checkable ingredients, servings scale and units convert (same kind only) offline, and the AI suggests substitutions per ingredient. Answers are remembered for the household, and one can be saved to the recipe's notes.

**Architecture:**
- **Display maths:** scaling, conversion and formatting are pure functions in `packages/domain/src/cooking.ts`, used only for display; the saved recipe never changes.
- **Cook mode:** a new route that reads the local database, keeps the cook's place in localStorage, and holds a screen wake lock.
- **Substitutions:** asked live through `POST /api/substitutions`, which reads the recipe as the user, checks the shared AI cap, calls Claude with a strict tool, validates, and upserts into a synced `substitutions` table only the API writes.

**Tech Stack:** Postgres 17 + RLS (dbmate), PowerSync sync streams, Hono API, `@anthropic-ai/sdk`, zod 4, Expo / React Native Web, Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-cook-mode-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Cook mode text:** body 20 px, step 24 px, tap targets at least 44 px (spec §3).
- **Cook place:** stored under the localStorage key `cook:<recipe id>`, expiring after **12 hours**, cleared by Done, by Reset and by sign-out wipe (spec §3).
- **Servings and scale:** servings 1–999. Without servings, the scale steps through ½, 1, 1½, 2, 3, 4 (spec §4).
- **US formatting:** fractions are ⅛ ¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ ⅞.
- **Metric formatting:** g and ml below 100 in whole units, 100–999 in steps of 5; kg and l from 1,000 with one decimal and no trailing ".0" (spec §4).
- **Counts:** none, clove, can, package, stick, slice, pinch and dash round to the nearest ½, with "≈" when rounding changed the value (spec §4).
- **Unit stepping:**
  - down: cup → tbsp below ¼ cup, tbsp → tsp below 1 tbsp, tsp → "a pinch of" below ⅛ tsp;
  - up: tsp → tbsp at 3, tbsp → cup at 16, g → kg at 1,000, ml → l at 1,000, oz → lb at 16 (spec §4).
- **Conversion factors:** tsp 4.929 ml, tbsp 14.787, cup 236.588, pint 473.176, quart 946.353, gallon 3785.41 ml; oz 28.3495 g, lb 453.592 g. "oz" is weight (spec §4).
- **Substitution answer:**
  - 0–3 options, each with `substitute` 1–200, `amount` 1–200, `how` 0–500 and `effect` 1–300 characters;
  - a `note` of 0–500 characters or null;
  - at least one option, or a note (spec §6).
- **`POST /api/substitutions` answers:** 401, 400, 403, 404, 402 `quota`, 503 `ai_unavailable`, 422 `invalid_output`, 200 `{ options, note, ingredient_raw }` (spec §6).
- **Copy, exactly:**
  - Cook mode: "Cook", "Serves 4" (format), "Scale ×1" (format), "As written", "US", "Metric", "Step 2 of 7" (format), "Previous", "Next", "Done", "Reset", "No steps", "Your screen may sleep while cooking.", "Amounts in steps are for the original 4 servings" (format), "Amounts in steps are for the original recipe".
  - Substitutions: "Substitute", "Ask for substitutes", "Ask again", "Finding substitutes…", "Substitutions need a connection.", "No substitutes saved yet.", "This was for 1 cup buttermilk" (format), "For the original amount", "Add to notes", "Notes are full".
  - Errors: "Over this month's AI limit", "Couldn't reach the AI service. Try again.", "Couldn't read the suggestions. Try again.", "Couldn't get substitutes. Try again." (spec §3, §7).
- **CLAUDE.md rules 1–8 apply:** RLS and the registry for the new table; sync mirrors RLS; AI output validated in `packages/domain`; prompts in versioned files; viewers can't write.
- **No real AI calls in CI.**
- **Never run `pnpm db:reset`** on the owner's machine. Seed rows are added by hand with `on conflict do nothing`.
- **After changing `powersync/sync-config.yaml`,** restart sync (`docker compose restart sync`); it reads the file only at start.

## Review Focus

1. **A recipe edited after its cook place was saved** (an ingredient removed, steps cut from 7 to 3). The cook expects cook mode to open, not crash or show "Step 6 of 3". Pinned in Task 6 (`fitPlace`).
2. **Ingredients the parser only half read** ("1 (14 oz) can tomatoes", "a handful of basil", "2 large eggs, beaten" with unit null). The cook expects the line to stay sensible when scaled, never "NaN" or a lost word. Pinned in Task 1.
3. **Scaling down to a tiny amount, or up to a large one** (¼ tsp salt at ×¼, 8 tbsp butter at ×2). The cook expects "a pinch of salt" and "1 cup butter", not "0.0625 tsp" or "16 tbsp". Pinned in Task 1.
4. **Substitutions saved for an ingredient line that was later edited or deleted.** The cook expects the old answer labelled as being for the old line, and no crash for a deleted line. Pinned in Task 2 (`isStale`) and Task 8 (the panel shows "This was for …").
5. **Add to notes on a recipe whose notes are nearly full.** The cook expects "Notes are full", not a failed sync upload. Pinned in Task 7.

## Decisions this plan makes inside the spec (for the executor's ledger)

- **The phone never writes `substitutions` locally.** Rows only arrive by sync, so no upload filtering is needed. The existing upload code sends an unknown table's op with no data, which the server refuses with `table_not_allowed`. Spec §7 says "lists no fields for it, so even a stray local write is never sent"; the stray write cannot happen. The API test still checks the refusal.
- **One shared usage helper.** The spend and month queries move into `apps/api/src/llm/usage.ts`, used by imports and substitutions alike.
- **An ask for a recipe in the Trash** answers 404 (spec §6 lists "in the Trash").
- **Scale steps for a recipe without servings** are stored as the factor in the cook place, so the recipe screen and cook mode agree.
- **"a pinch of {item}"** is the wording when a scaled teaspoon amount falls below ⅛ tsp (spec §4 says "a pinch").

```ts
import type { Ingredient, Unit } from './ingredients';

/** FR-23: how amounts are shown. Display only; the saved recipe never changes. */
export type UnitSystem = 'as_written' | 'us' | 'metric';
export type Amount = { value: number; max: number | null; unit: Unit | null };

/** FR-22: scale steps for a recipe without servings. */
export const SCALE_STEPS = [0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4] as const;
const SERVINGS = [1, 999] as const;

const ML: Partial<Record<Unit, number>> = {
  tsp: 4.929,
  tbsp: 14.787,
  cup: 236.588,
  pint: 473.176,
  quart: 946.353,
  gallon: 3785.41,
  ml: 1,
  l: 1000,
};
const GRAMS: Partial<Record<Unit, number>> = { oz: 28.3495, lb: 453.592, g: 1, kg: 1000 };
const US_UNITS = new Set<Unit>(['tsp', 'tbsp', 'cup', 'pint', 'quart', 'gallon', 'oz', 'lb']);
const METRIC_UNITS = new Set<Unit>(['ml', 'l', 'g', 'kg']);
const PLURAL: Partial<Record<Unit, string>> = {
  cup: 'cups',
  pint: 'pints',
  quart: 'quarts',
  gallon: 'gallons',
  clove: 'cloves',
  can: 'cans',
  package: 'packages',
  stick: 'sticks',
  slice: 'slices',
  pinch: 'pinches',
  dash: 'dashes',
};
const EIGHTHS: [number, string][] = [
  [0, ''],
  [1 / 8, '⅛'],
  [1 / 4, '¼'],
  [1 / 3, '⅓'],
  [1 / 2, '½'],
  [2 / 3, '⅔'],
  [3 / 4, '¾'],
  [7 / 8, '⅞'],
  [1, ''],
];

export function scaleFactor(
  recipeServings: number | null,
  chosenServings: number | null,
  scale: number,
): number {
  if (recipeServings && chosenServings) return chosenServings / recipeServings;
  return recipeServings ? 1 : scale;
}

export function stepServings(value: number, delta: 1 | -1): number {
  return Math.min(SERVINGS[1], Math.max(SERVINGS[0], Math.round(value) + delta));
}

/** The next scale step up or down from `factor` (snapping a value between steps). */
export function stepScale(factor: number, delta: 1 | -1): number {
  const steps: readonly number[] = SCALE_STEPS;
  if (delta > 0) return steps.find((step) => step > factor + 1e-9) ?? steps[steps.length - 1]!;
  return [...steps].reverse().find((step) => step < factor - 1e-9) ?? steps[0]!;
}

export function scaleIngredient(ingredient: Ingredient, factor: number): Ingredient {
  if (ingredient.heading || ingredient.quantity === null || factor === 1) return ingredient;
  return {
    ...ingredient,
    quantity: ingredient.quantity * factor,
    quantity_max: ingredient.quantity_max === null ? null : ingredient.quantity_max * factor,
  };
}

function rescale(amount: Amount, ratio: number, unit: Unit): Amount {
  return { value: amount.value * ratio, max: amount.max === null ? null : amount.max * ratio, unit };
}

/** Same kind only: volume ↔ volume, weight ↔ weight. Counted units never convert. */
export function convertAmount(amount: Amount, system: UnitSystem): Amount {
  const unit = amount.unit;
  if (system === 'as_written' || unit === null) return amount;
  if (system === 'metric' && US_UNITS.has(unit)) {
    const volume = ML[unit];
    if (volume) return stepUnits(rescale(amount, volume, 'ml')) as Amount;
    return stepUnits(rescale(amount, GRAMS[unit]!, 'g')) as Amount;
  }
  if (system === 'us' && METRIC_UNITS.has(unit)) {
    const volume = ML[unit];
    if (volume) {
      const ml = amount.value * volume;
      const target: Unit = ml / ML.cup! >= 0.25 ? 'cup' : ml / ML.tbsp! >= 0.25 ? 'tbsp' : 'tsp';
      return rescale(amount, volume / ML[target]!, target);
    }
    const grams = amount.value * GRAMS[unit]!;
    const target: Unit = grams / GRAMS.lb! >= 1 ? 'lb' : 'oz';
    return rescale(amount, GRAMS[unit]! / GRAMS[target]!, target);
  }
  return amount;
}

/** Spec §4: tiny amounts step down a unit, large ones step up. */
export function stepUnits(amount: Amount): Amount | 'pinch' {
  let current = amount;
  // Multiply going down a unit, divide going up: exact for these ratios.
  const down = (times: number, unit: Unit) => (current = rescale(current, times, unit));
  const up = (per: number, unit: Unit) =>
    (current = {
      value: current.value / per,
      max: current.max === null ? null : current.max / per,
      unit,
    });
  if (current.unit === 'cup' && current.value < 0.25) down(16, 'tbsp');
  if (current.unit === 'tbsp' && current.value < 1) down(3, 'tsp');
  if (current.unit === 'tsp' && current.value < 0.125) return 'pinch';
  if (current.unit === 'tsp' && current.value >= 3) up(3, 'tbsp');
  if (current.unit === 'tbsp' && current.value >= 16) up(16, 'cup');
  if (current.unit === 'g' && current.value >= 1000) up(1000, 'kg');
  if (current.unit === 'ml' && current.value >= 1000) up(1000, 'l');
  if (current.unit === 'oz' && current.value >= 16) up(16, 'lb');
  return current;
}

function fraction(value: number): string {
  let whole = Math.floor(value);
  const rest = value - whole;
  let [best, glyph] = EIGHTHS[0]!;
  for (const [step, text] of EIGHTHS) {
    if (Math.abs(rest - step) < Math.abs(rest - best)) [best, glyph] = [step, text];
  }
  if (best === 1) whole += 1;
  if (whole === 0 && glyph === '') return '⅛';
  return `${whole > 0 ? whole : ''}${glyph}`;
}

/** An amount as a cook writes it (spec §4). */
export function formatAmount(value: number, unit: Unit | null): string {
  if (unit !== null && US_UNITS.has(unit)) return fraction(value);
  if (unit === 'g' || unit === 'ml') {
    const rounded = value < 100 ? Math.max(1, Math.round(value)) : Math.round(value / 5) * 5;
    return String(rounded);
  }
  if (unit === 'kg' || unit === 'l') return String(Math.round(value * 10) / 10);
  // Counts: eggs, cloves, cans and the like.
  const rounded = Math.max(0.5, Math.round(value * 2) / 2);
  const text = `${Math.floor(rounded) > 0 ? Math.floor(rounded) : ''}${rounded % 1 ? '½' : ''}`;
  return Math.abs(rounded - value) > 1e-6 ? `≈${text}` : text;
}

// Plural unless the shown amount is one or less: "⅔ cup", "1 cup", "1½ cups".
function unitLabel(unit: Unit, shown: string): string {
  return /^≈?(1|⅛|¼|⅓|½|⅔|¾|⅞)$/.test(shown) ? unit : (PLURAL[unit] ?? unit);
}

/** FR-21–23: the line to show for a scale and unit system. */
export function displayIngredient(
  ingredient: Ingredient,
  options: { factor: number; units: UnitSystem },
): string {
  if (ingredient.heading || ingredient.quantity === null) return ingredient.raw;
  if (options.factor === 1 && options.units === 'as_written') return ingredient.raw;
  const scaled = scaleIngredient(ingredient, options.factor);
  const converted = convertAmount(
    { value: scaled.quantity!, max: scaled.quantity_max, unit: scaled.unit },
    options.units,
  );
  const stepped = converted.unit !== null && (US_UNITS.has(converted.unit) || METRIC_UNITS.has(converted.unit))
    ? stepUnits(converted)
    : converted;
  const note = ingredient.note ? `, ${ingredient.note}` : '';
  if (stepped === 'pinch') return `a pinch of ${ingredient.item}${note}`;
  const value = formatAmount(stepped.value, stepped.unit);
  const amount = stepped.max === null ? value : `${value}–${formatAmount(stepped.max, stepped.unit)}`;
  if (!ingredient.item) {
    // Parsed an amount but no item: swap only the leading number of the typed line.
    return ingredient.raw.replace(/^\s*[\d½¼¾⅓⅔⅛⅜⅝⅞.,/\s-]+/, `${amount} `);
  }
  const unit = stepped.unit ? ` ${unitLabel(stepped.unit, stepped.max === null ? value : 'range')}` : '';
  return `${amount}${unit} ${ingredient.item}${note}`;
}
```

```ts
import { z } from 'zod';

/** FR-24: what the phone sends to ask. The id is the row's, generated on the phone. */
export const substitutionAskSchema = z.strictObject({
  id: z.guid(),
  recipe_id: z.guid(),
  ingredient_id: z.guid(),
});
export type SubstitutionAsk = z.infer<typeof substitutionAskSchema>;

export const substitutionOptionSchema = z.strictObject({
  substitute: z.string().trim().min(1).max(200),
  amount: z.string().trim().min(1).max(200),
  how: z.string().trim().max(500),
  effect: z.string().trim().min(1).max(300),
});
export type SubstitutionOption = z.infer<typeof substitutionOptionSchema>;

export const substitutionAnswerSchema = z
  .strictObject({
    options: z.array(substitutionOptionSchema).max(3),
    note: z.string().trim().max(500).nullable(),
  })
  .refine((answer) => answer.options.length > 0 || (answer.note ?? '') !== '', {
    message: 'An answer needs an option or a note',
  });
export type SubstitutionAnswer = z.infer<typeof substitutionAnswerSchema>;

const text = (value: unknown) => (typeof value === 'string' ? value.trim() : value);

/** The model's answer, untrusted (rule 7): tidied, then held to the schema. */
export function toSubstitutionAnswer(input: unknown): SubstitutionAnswer | null {
  if (typeof input !== 'object' || input === null) return null;
  const { options, note } = input as { options?: unknown; note?: unknown };
  if (!Array.isArray(options)) return null;
  const tidied = {
    options: options.slice(0, 3).map((option) =>
      typeof option === 'object' && option !== null
        ? Object.fromEntries(Object.entries(option).map(([key, value]) => [key, text(value)]))
        : option,
    ),
    note: typeof note === 'string' && note.trim() !== '' ? note.trim() : null,
  };
  const parsed = substitutionAnswerSchema.safeParse(tidied);
  return parsed.success ? parsed.data : null;
}

/** The answer was for a line that has since changed, or is gone. */
export function isStale(row: { ingredient_raw: string }, ingredient: { raw: string } | undefined): boolean {
  return !ingredient || ingredient.raw.trim() !== row.ingredient_raw.trim();
}

/** FR-25: the line added to the recipe's notes. */
export function substitutionNoteLine(ingredientRaw: string, option: SubstitutionOption): string {
  const amount = option.amount.toLowerCase();
  const named = option.substitute
    .toLowerCase()
    .split(/[^\p{L}]+/u)
    .some((word) => word.length > 3 && amount.includes(word));
  const what = named ? option.amount : `${option.amount} (${option.substitute})`;
  return `Instead of ${ingredientRaw.trim()}: ${what} — ${option.effect}`;
}
```

```ts
import type Anthropic from '@anthropic-ai/sdk';
import type { Ingredient } from '@recipe-app/domain';
import type { ParseRequest } from './parseRecipe';
import prompt from './prompts/suggest-substitutes.v1.md';

export const SUBSTITUTE_PROMPT_VERSION = 'suggest-substitutes.v1';
const RECIPE_MAX = 20_000;

export type SubstituteRecipe = {
  title: string;
  servings: number | null;
  ingredients: Ingredient[];
  steps: { text: string }[];
};

/** The strict tool whose input is the answer (spec §6). Still validated (rule 7). */
export const RECORD_SUBSTITUTES_TOOL: Anthropic.Tool = {
  name: 'record_substitutes',
  description: 'Record the substitutes for the marked ingredient. Always call this tool exactly once.',
  strict: true,
  input_schema: {
    type: 'object',
    additionalProperties: false,
    properties: {
      options: {
        type: 'array',
        items: {
          type: 'object',
          additionalProperties: false,
          properties: {
            substitute: { type: 'string' },
            amount: { type: 'string' },
            how: { type: 'string' },
            effect: { type: 'string' },
          },
          required: ['substitute', 'amount', 'how', 'effect'],
        },
      },
      note: { type: ['string', 'null'] },
    },
    required: ['options', 'note'],
  },
};

export function buildSubstituteRequest(
  recipe: SubstituteRecipe,
  ingredientId: string,
): ParseRequest & { user: string } {
  const target = recipe.ingredients.find((line) => line.id === ingredientId);
  const lines = recipe.ingredients.map(
    (line) => `- ${line.raw}${line.id === ingredientId ? '  ← replace this' : ''}`,
  );
  const body = [
    `Title: ${recipe.title}`,
    `Servings: ${recipe.servings ?? 'not given'}`,
    'Ingredients:',
    ...lines,
    'Steps:',
    ...recipe.steps.map((step, index) => `${index + 1}. ${step.text}`),
  ]
    .join('\n')
    .slice(0, RECIPE_MAX)
    // The recipe must not be able to end its own block and talk to the model.
    .replace(/<\/recipe>/gi, '</ recipe>');
  const user = ['<recipe>', body, '</recipe>', `Ingredient to replace: ${target?.raw ?? ''}`].join('\n');
  return { system: prompt, user };
}
```

```ts
import {
  toSubstitutionAnswer,
  type Ingredient,
  type SubstitutionAnswer,
  type SubstitutionAsk,
} from '@recipe-app/domain';
import type { TokenClaims, VerifyToken } from '../auth/verify';
import type { AsClaims } from '../db/asUser';
import { AiUnavailableError, costMicroUsd, type AiConfig, type Gateway } from '../llm/gateway';
import { buildSubstituteRequest, RECORD_SUBSTITUTES_TOOL } from '../llm/suggestSubstitutes';
import { monthSpentMicroUsd, recordSpend } from '../llm/usage';

export type SubstitutionOutcome =
  | { kind: 'answer'; answer: SubstitutionAnswer; ingredient_raw: string }
  | { kind: 'forbidden' | 'missing' | 'quota' | 'ai_unavailable' | 'invalid_output' };
export type SubstitutionService = {
  ask(token: string, ask: SubstitutionAsk): Promise<SubstitutionOutcome>;
};

type RecipeRow = {
  title: string;
  servings: number | null;
  ingredients: Ingredient[] | null;
  steps: { text: string }[] | null;
};
const WRITERS = ['admin', 'member'];

/**
 * FR-24: asks Claude about one ingredient and remembers the answer for the
 * household. Every statement runs as the user (rule 1); the token is checked
 * once, so a slow answer still lands after the phone has gone.
 */
export function createSubstitutionService(deps: {
  verify: VerifyToken;
  asClaims: AsClaims;
  gateway: Gateway;
  ai: Pick<AiConfig, 'capUsd' | 'priceInputPerMTokUsd' | 'priceOutputPerMTokUsd'>;
  log?: (event: Record<string, unknown>) => void;
}): SubstitutionService {
  return {
    async ask(token, ask) {
      const claims: TokenClaims = await deps.verify(token);
      if (!WRITERS.includes(String(claims.household_role))) return { kind: 'forbidden' };

      const found = await deps.asClaims(claims, async (tx) => {
        const [recipe] = await tx<RecipeRow[]>`
          select title, servings, ingredients, steps from public.recipes
          where id = ${ask.recipe_id} and deleted_at is null`;
        return { recipe, spent: await monthSpentMicroUsd(tx) };
      });
      const ingredients = found.recipe?.ingredients ?? [];
      const line = ingredients.find((item) => item.id === ask.ingredient_id && !item.heading);
      if (!found.recipe || !line) return { kind: 'missing' };
      if (found.spent >= Math.round(deps.ai.capUsd * 1_000_000)) return { kind: 'quota' };

      let call;
      try {
        call = await deps.gateway.callTool(
          buildSubstituteRequest(
            { ...found.recipe, ingredients, steps: found.recipe.steps ?? [] },
            ask.ingredient_id,
          ),
          RECORD_SUBSTITUTES_TOOL,
        );
      } catch (error) {
        if (!(error instanceof AiUnavailableError)) throw error;
        deps.log?.({ event: 'ai_unavailable', recipe: ask.recipe_id, message: error.message });
        return { kind: 'ai_unavailable' };
      }
      const answer = toSubstitutionAnswer(call.input);
      await deps.asClaims(claims, async (tx) => {
        await recordSpend(tx, costMicroUsd(call.usage, deps.ai));
        if (!answer) return;
        // Asking again replaces the remembered answer for this line.
        await tx`
          insert into public.substitutions
            (id, household_id, recipe_id, ingredient_id, ingredient_raw, options, note)
          values (${ask.id}, (select public.auth_household_id()), ${ask.recipe_id},
                  ${ask.ingredient_id}, ${line.raw}, ${tx.json(answer.options as never)}, ${answer.note})
          on conflict (recipe_id, ingredient_id) where deleted_at is null
          do update set ingredient_raw = excluded.ingredient_raw,
                        options = excluded.options, note = excluded.note`;
      });
      return answer ? { kind: 'answer', answer, ingredient_raw: line.raw } : { kind: 'invalid_output' };
    },
  };
}
```

```ts
import type { UnitSystem } from '@recipe-app/domain';

/** Spec §3: this cook's place in a recipe, on this phone only. */
export type CookPlace = {
  ticked: string[];
  step: number;
  /** Chosen servings, when the recipe has servings. */
  servings: number | null;
  /** The scale, when the recipe has no servings. */
  scale: number;
  units: UnitSystem;
  at: number;
};

export const PLACE_TTL_MS = 12 * 60 * 60 * 1000;
export const PLACE_PREFIX = 'cook:';
const UNITS: readonly string[] = ['as_written', 'us', 'metric'];

type Store = Pick<Storage, 'getItem' | 'setItem' | 'removeItem'>;
type ListStore = Pick<Storage, 'key' | 'removeItem'> & { readonly length: number };

export function freshPlace(servings: number | null, now: number): CookPlace {
  return { ticked: [], step: 0, servings, scale: 1, units: 'as_written', at: now };
}

function isPlace(value: unknown): value is CookPlace {
  const place = value as Partial<CookPlace> | null;
  return (
    typeof place === 'object' &&
    place !== null &&
    Array.isArray(place.ticked) &&
    place.ticked.every((id) => typeof id === 'string') &&
    Number.isInteger(place.step) &&
    (place.servings === null || typeof place.servings === 'number') &&
    typeof place.scale === 'number' &&
    UNITS.includes(String(place.units)) &&
    typeof place.at === 'number'
  );
}

export function readPlace(store: Store, recipeId: string, now: number): CookPlace | null {
  const key = PLACE_PREFIX + recipeId;
  let value: unknown;
  try {
    value = JSON.parse(store.getItem(key) ?? 'null');
  } catch {
    return null;
  }
  if (!isPlace(value)) return null;
  if (now - value.at > PLACE_TTL_MS) {
    store.removeItem(key);
    return null;
  }
  return value;
}

export function writePlace(store: Store, recipeId: string, place: CookPlace): void {
  store.setItem(PLACE_PREFIX + recipeId, JSON.stringify(place));
}

export function clearPlace(store: Store, recipeId: string): void {
  store.removeItem(PLACE_PREFIX + recipeId);
}

/** Sign-out wipe: no cook's place outlives the session's data. */
export function clearAllPlaces(store: ListStore): void {
  const keys: string[] = [];
  for (let i = 0; i < store.length; i++) {
    const key = store.key(i);
    if (key?.startsWith(PLACE_PREFIX)) keys.push(key);
  }
  for (const key of keys) store.removeItem(key);
}

/** A place saved before the recipe was edited, made to fit it again. */
export function fitPlace(
  place: CookPlace,
  recipe: { servings: number | null; ingredients: { id: string }[]; steps: unknown[] },
): CookPlace {
  const ids = new Set(recipe.ingredients.map((ingredient) => ingredient.id));
  return {
    ...place,
    ticked: place.ticked.filter((id) => ids.has(id)),
    step: Math.max(0, Math.min(place.step, recipe.steps.length - 1)),
    servings: recipe.servings === null ? null : (place.servings ?? recipe.servings),
  };
}
```

```ts
import { useCallback, useState } from 'react';
import type { RecipeRecord } from '../recipes/rows';
import { clearPlace, fitPlace, freshPlace, readPlace, writePlace, type CookPlace } from './place';

const store = () => globalThis.localStorage;

/**
 * The cook's place for a recipe, shared by the recipe screen and cook mode
 * through localStorage. Each change is saved at once, so a lock or reload
 * comes back to the same ticks, step, servings and units.
 */
export function useCookPlace(recipe: RecipeRecord): {
  place: CookPlace;
  update(change: Partial<CookPlace>): void;
  reset(): void;
} {
  const [place, setPlace] = useState<CookPlace>(() => {
    const saved = store() ? readPlace(store(), recipe.id, Date.now()) : null;
    return fitPlace(saved ?? freshPlace(recipe.servings, Date.now()), recipe);
  });
  const update = useCallback(
    (change: Partial<CookPlace>) => {
      setPlace((current) => {
        const next = { ...current, ...change, at: Date.now() };
        if (store()) writePlace(store(), recipe.id, next);
        return next;
      });
    },
    [recipe.id],
  );
  const reset = useCallback(() => {
    if (store()) clearPlace(store(), recipe.id);
    setPlace(freshPlace(recipe.servings, Date.now()));
  }, [recipe.id, recipe.servings]);
  return { place, update, reset };
}
```

```ts
import { useEffect, useState } from 'react';

type Sentinel = { release(): Promise<void> };
type WakeLockNavigator = Navigator & { wakeLock?: { request(type: 'screen'): Promise<Sentinel> } };

/**
 * FR-21: keeps the screen on while `active`. The browser drops the lock
 * when the page is hidden, so it is asked for again on return.
 */
export function useWakeLock(active: boolean): 'on' | 'unsupported' {
  const [state, setState] = useState<'on' | 'unsupported'>('on');
  useEffect(() => {
    const wakeLock = (globalThis.navigator as WakeLockNavigator | undefined)?.wakeLock;
    if (!active) return;
    if (!wakeLock) {
      setState('unsupported');
      return;
    }
    let sentinel: Sentinel | null = null;
    let live = true;
    const request = () => {
      if (document.visibilityState !== 'visible') return;
      wakeLock.request('screen').then(
        (held) => {
          if (live) sentinel = held;
          else void held.release();
          setState('on');
        },
        () => setState('unsupported'),
      );
    };
    request();
    document.addEventListener('visibilitychange', request);
    return () => {
      live = false;
      document.removeEventListener('visibilitychange', request);
      void sentinel?.release();
    };
  }, [active]);
  return state;
}
```

```ts
import { toSubstitutionAnswer, type SubstitutionAnswer } from '@recipe-app/domain';

export type SubstitutionRecord = {
  id: string;
  recipe_id: string;
  ingredient_id: string;
  ingredient_raw: string;
  answer: SubstitutionAnswer;
};

/** A synced row, or null when it cannot be read (an older or newer server). */
export function substitutionFromRow(row: Record<string, unknown>): SubstitutionRecord | null {
  const { id, recipe_id, ingredient_id, ingredient_raw } = row;
  if ([id, recipe_id, ingredient_id, ingredient_raw].some((value) => typeof value !== 'string')) return null;
  let options: unknown;
  try {
    options = typeof row.options === 'string' ? JSON.parse(row.options) : row.options;
  } catch {
    return null;
  }
  const answer = toSubstitutionAnswer({ options, note: row.note ?? null });
  if (!answer) return null;
  return {
    id: id as string,
    recipe_id: recipe_id as string,
    ingredient_id: ingredient_id as string,
    ingredient_raw: ingredient_raw as string,
    answer,
  };
}
```

```ts
import type { AbstractPowerSyncDatabase } from '@powersync/web';
import { RECIPE_LIMITS } from '@recipe-app/domain';
import { canWrite, type Claims } from '../session/claims';

type Db = Pick<AbstractPowerSyncDatabase, 'execute' | 'getOptional'>;

export const SUBSTITUTIONS_SQL =
  'select * from substitutions where recipe_id = ? and deleted_at is null';

/** FR-25: the chosen substitute kept as a line in the recipe's notes. */
export async function addSubstitutionToNotes(
  db: Db,
  claims: Claims,
  recipeId: string,
  line: string,
): Promise<'added' | 'full' | 'missing'> {
  if (!canWrite(claims.role)) throw new Error('Viewers cannot change recipes');
  const row = await db.getOptional<{ notes: string | null }>('select notes from recipes where id = ?', [
    recipeId,
  ]);
  if (!row) return 'missing';
  const current = (row.notes ?? '').trimEnd();
  const notes = current ? `${current}\n${line}` : line;
  if (notes.length > RECIPE_LIMITS.notes) return 'full';
  await db.execute('update recipes set notes = ? where id = ?', [notes, recipeId]);
  return 'added';
}
```

```ts
import { toSubstitutionAnswer, type SubstitutionAnswer, type SubstitutionAsk } from '@recipe-app/domain';
import type { ApiDeps } from '../imports/api';

export type AskResult =
  | { kind: 'answer'; answer: SubstitutionAnswer; ingredient_raw: string }
  | { kind: 'error'; message: string };

const MESSAGES: Record<string, string> = {
  quota: "Over this month's AI limit",
  ai_unavailable: "Couldn't reach the AI service. Try again.",
  invalid_output: "Couldn't read the suggestions. Try again.",
};
const FALLBACK = "Couldn't get substitutes. Try again.";

/** FR-24: asks the server for substitutes. Never throws. */
export async function askSubstitutes(ask: SubstitutionAsk, deps: ApiDeps): Promise<AskResult> {
  try {
    const response = await deps.fetch(`${deps.apiUrl}/substitutions`, {
      method: 'POST',
      headers: { 'Content-Type': 'application/json', Authorization: `Bearer ${deps.token}` },
      body: JSON.stringify(ask),
    });
    const body = (await response.json().catch(() => null)) as
      | { error?: string; ingredient_raw?: unknown; options?: unknown; note?: unknown }
      | null;
    if (!response.ok) return { kind: 'error', message: MESSAGES[body?.error ?? ''] ?? FALLBACK };
    const answer = toSubstitutionAnswer({ options: body?.options, note: body?.note ?? null });
    if (!answer || typeof body?.ingredient_raw !== 'string') {
      return { kind: 'error', message: MESSAGES.invalid_output! };
    }
    return { kind: 'answer', answer, ingredient_raw: body.ingredient_raw };
  } catch {
    return { kind: 'error', message: FALLBACK };
  }
}
```

```tsx
import { usePowerSync } from '@powersync/react';
import { isStale, substitutionNoteLine, type Ingredient, type SubstitutionAnswer } from '@recipe-app/domain';
import { useState } from 'react';
import { ActivityIndicator, Pressable, Text, View } from 'react-native';
import { useNetworkOnline } from '../db/useNetworkOnline';
import { withSession } from '../imports/api';
import { canWrite, type Claims } from '../session/claims';
import { askSubstitutes } from '../substitutions/api';
import { addSubstitutionToNotes } from '../substitutions/repository';
import type { SubstitutionRecord } from '../substitutions/rows';

const link = { textDecorationLine: 'underline' } as const;

/** FR-24, FR-25: substitutes for one ingredient, remembered or asked for. */
export function SubstitutePanel({
  claims,
  recipeId,
  ingredient,
  remembered,
  scaled,
}: {
  claims: Claims;
  recipeId: string;
  ingredient: Ingredient;
  remembered: SubstitutionRecord | null;
  scaled: boolean;
}) {
  const db = usePowerSync();
  const online = useNetworkOnline();
  const writer = canWrite(claims.role);
  const [asking, setAsking] = useState(false);
  const [error, setError] = useState<string | null>(null);
  const [notice, setNotice] = useState<string | null>(null);
  // The answer just returned shows at once; the synced row agrees shortly.
  const [fresh, setFresh] = useState<{ answer: SubstitutionAnswer; raw: string } | null>(null);
  const answer = fresh?.answer ?? remembered?.answer ?? null;
  const askedFor = fresh?.raw ?? remembered?.ingredient_raw ?? ingredient.raw;
  const stale = remembered !== null && fresh === null && isStale(remembered, ingredient);

  async function ask() {
    setAsking(true);
    setError(null);
    const result = await withSession((deps) =>
      askSubstitutes({ id: crypto.randomUUID(), recipe_id: recipeId, ingredient_id: ingredient.id }, deps),
    );
    setAsking(false);
    if (!result) setError("Couldn't get substitutes. Try again.");
    else if (result.kind === 'error') setError(result.message);
    else setFresh({ answer: result.answer, raw: result.ingredient_raw });
  }

  async function addToNotes(line: string) {
    const outcome = await addSubstitutionToNotes(db, claims, recipeId, line);
    setNotice(outcome === 'added' ? 'Added to notes' : outcome === 'full' ? 'Notes are full' : null);
  }

  return (
    <View style={{ gap: 8, backgroundColor: '#f6f6f2', borderRadius: 8, padding: 10 }}>
      {answer ? (
        <>
          {stale ? <Text style={{ color: '#7a5b00' }}>This was for {askedFor}</Text> : null}
          {scaled ? <Text style={{ color: '#555' }}>For the original amount</Text> : null}
          {answer.options.map((option, index) => (
            <View key={index} style={{ gap: 2 }}>
              <Text style={{ fontWeight: '600' }}>{option.substitute}</Text>
              <Text>{option.amount}</Text>
              {option.how ? <Text style={{ color: '#444' }}>{option.how}</Text> : null}
              <Text style={{ color: '#444' }}>{option.effect}</Text>
              {writer ? (
                <Pressable role="button" onPress={() => void addToNotes(substitutionNoteLine(askedFor, option))}>
                  <Text style={link}>Add to notes</Text>
                </Pressable>
              ) : null}
            </View>
          ))}
          {answer.note ? <Text>{answer.note}</Text> : null}
        </>
      ) : null}
      {asking ? (
        <View style={{ flexDirection: 'row', gap: 8, alignItems: 'center' }}>
          <ActivityIndicator size="small" />
          <Text>Finding substitutes…</Text>
        </View>
      ) : writer && online ? (
        <Pressable role="button" onPress={() => void ask()} style={{ minHeight: 44, justifyContent: 'center' }}>
          <Text style={{ ...link, fontWeight: '600' }}>{answer ? 'Ask again' : 'Ask for substitutes'}</Text>
        </Pressable>
      ) : !answer ? (
        <Text>{writer ? 'Substitutions need a connection.' : 'No substitutes saved yet.'}</Text>
      ) : null}
      {error ? (
        <Text role="alert" style={{ color: '#b00020' }}>
          {error}
        </Text>
      ) : null}
      {notice ? <Text aria-live="polite">{notice}</Text> : null}
    </View>
  );
}
```

```tsx
/** FR-21: cooking at the counter: awake, large, one step at a time. */
export function CookScreen({ claims, id }: { claims: Claims; id: string }) {
  const { recipe } = useRecipe(id, claims.userId);
  if (recipe === undefined) return null;
  if (recipe === null || recipe.deleted_at) {
    return <Text style={{ padding: 16 }}>This recipe could not be read.</Text>;
  }
  return <Cooking claims={claims} recipe={recipe} />;
}

function Cooking({ claims, recipe }: { claims: Claims; recipe: RecipeRecord }) {
  const { place, update, reset } = useCookPlace(recipe);
  const wake = useWakeLock(true);
  const substitutions = useSubstitutions(recipe.id);
  const factor = scaleFactor(recipe.servings, place.servings, place.scale);
  const steps = recipe.steps;
  const last = steps.length - 1;
  const toggle = (ingredientId: string) =>
    update({
      ticked: place.ticked.includes(ingredientId)
        ? place.ticked.filter((tick) => tick !== ingredientId)
        : [...place.ticked, ingredientId],
    });
  const done = () => {
    reset();
    router.replace(`/recipe/${recipe.id}`);
  };

  return (
    <ScrollView contentContainerStyle={{ padding: 16, gap: 16, maxWidth: 720 }}>
      <View style={{ flexDirection: 'row', justifyContent: 'space-between', alignItems: 'center' }}>
        <Pressable role="button" onPress={() => router.replace(`/recipe/${recipe.id}`)} style={{ minHeight: 44, justifyContent: 'center' }}>
          <Text style={{ fontSize: 18, textDecorationLine: 'underline' }}>{recipe.title}</Text>
        </Pressable>
        <Pressable role="button" onPress={reset} style={{ minHeight: 44, justifyContent: 'center' }}>
          <Text style={{ fontSize: 18, textDecorationLine: 'underline' }}>Reset</Text>
        </Pressable>
      </View>
      {wake === 'unsupported' ? <Text style={{ color: '#555' }}>Your screen may sleep while cooking.</Text> : null}
      <ServingsBar recipeServings={recipe.servings} place={place} onChange={update} large />
      <IngredientList
        claims={claims}
        recipeId={recipe.id}
        ingredients={recipe.ingredients}
        factor={factor}
        units={place.units}
        substitutions={substitutions}
        checkable
        ticked={place.ticked}
        onToggle={toggle}
        large
      />
      <View style={{ gap: 12, borderTopWidth: 1, borderColor: '#ddd', paddingTop: 12 }}>
        {steps.length === 0 ? (
          <Text style={{ fontSize: 20 }}>No steps</Text>
        ) : (
          <>
            <Text style={{ fontSize: 18, color: '#444' }}>
              Step {place.step + 1} of {steps.length}
            </Text>
            <Text style={{ fontSize: 24, lineHeight: 34 }}>{steps[place.step]?.text}</Text>
            {factor !== 1 ? (

                {recipe.servings !== null
```

```markdown
# 14. Cook mode on the phone; substitutions asked live and remembered

Date: 2026-10-17 · Status: Accepted

## Context

Cooking from the phone (FR-21) must work offline at the counter, with servings scaled (FR-22) and units converted (FR-23). Substitutions (FR-24) need the AI, but are wanted at once; the same question asked again should cost nothing and work offline.

## Decision

- **Display-only maths.** Scaling, conversion and formatting are pure functions in `packages/domain/src/cooking.ts`, used only for display. The saved recipe never changes.
  - Conversion is between units of the same kind only: volume ↔ volume and weight ↔ weight, with US customary factors. Counted units never convert.
  - Amounts written inside step text are not changed; a note says so.
- **Cook mode lives on the phone.** It reads only the local database. The cook's place (ticks, step, servings, units) is kept in localStorage per recipe for 12 hours and cleared on sign-out. The screen is held awake with the Screen Wake Lock API, asked for again whenever the page becomes visible.
- **Substitutions are asked live and remembered.**
  - `POST /api/substitutions` reads the recipe as the user, checks the shared AI cap (`ai_usage`) and calls Claude with a strict `record_substitutes` tool.
  - It validates the answer with zod and upserts it into the synced `substitutions` table, one row per recipe ingredient.
  - Only the API writes that table; phones read it, so the answer appears on every household device, offline too.
  - Admins and members may ask; viewers read.

## Alternatives considered

- **Volume ↔ weight with a density table, or conversion by AI:** approximate or variable, and the AI needs a connection and costs every time.
- **Substitutions not stored:** the same question costs again, and never works offline.
- **Substitutions queued like imports:** works offline, but answers are wanted now, at the stove.

## Consequences

- A remembered answer can describe a line that has since been edited; the app labels it "This was for …" and offers Ask again.
- The phone shows amounts the cook did not type (scaled or converted). Rounding rules are in the domain tests.
- The wake lock needs iOS 16.4+ in Safari, and 18.4+ in a home-screen app; where it is missing, cook mode says the screen may sleep.
```
