omg making this tonight!! 😍 ok so for everyone asking: you need 2 lbs chicken thighs (boneless), a cup of plain yogurt, 2 tbsp garam masala, 4 cloves garlic minced, a thumb of ginger grated and a can of crushed tomatoes. marinate the chicken in the yogurt + half the spices for at least an hour. sear it in a hot pan, then add garlic ginger rest of the spices and tomatoes and simmer 25 min. finish with a splash of cream if u want. serves like 4-6 lol
