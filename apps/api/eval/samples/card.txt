Grandma's Dinner Rolls
Serves 12
1 pkg active dry yeast
Dissolve yeast in warm milk with the sugar, let stand 10 minutes.
1 cup warm milk
2 tbsp sugar
Stir in butter, egg and salt, then the flour a cup at a time.
1/4 cup butter, softened
1 egg
1 tsp salt
4 cups flour
Knead 10 min. Rise until doubled, about 1 hour. Shape 12 rolls.
Bake at 375°F for 15 to 18 minutes.
