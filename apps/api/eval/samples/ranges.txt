Weeknight Pasta with Garlic Sauce (serves 4, 30 minutes)

For the pasta:
1 lb spaghetti
1 tbsp salt for the water

For the sauce:
2-3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
½ tsp red pepper flakes
⅓ cup olive oil
1 to 2 cups pasta water
Handful of parsley, chopped

Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente. Meanwhile warm the oil and garlic over low heat
until fragrant, then add the pepper flakes. Toss the drained pasta in the oil with enough pasta
water to make it glossy. Finish with parsley.
