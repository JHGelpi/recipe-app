Chicken Rice Bowls
Serves 4

1 lb chicken breast, diced
1 cup jasmine rice
2 cups broccoli florets
2 tbsp soy sauce

1. Cook the rice.
2. Sear the chicken until cooked through.
3. Steam the broccoli and toss everything with soy sauce.

Nutrition per serving: 420 calories, 32 g protein, 45 g carbohydrates, 11 g fat
