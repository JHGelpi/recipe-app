The Best Lemon Bars You Will Ever Make

Last spring my neighbour brought over a bag of lemons from her tree, and I have been making these bars ever since. They are tart, buttery and very easy.

Before we get to the recipe, a few tips: use fresh lemons, and let the bars cool completely before cutting.

Lemon Bars

Makes 16 bars · Prep 20 min · Bake 45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 cups flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup lemon juice
  • Zest of 2 lemons

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Press the butter, powdered sugar and flour into a 9x13 pan.
  2. Bake the crust for 20 minutes.
  3. Whisk the eggs, sugar, lemon juice and zest; pour over the hot crust.
  4. Bake 25 minutes more, cool, dust with powdered sugar and cut.
